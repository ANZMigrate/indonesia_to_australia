Evidence before a visa category

Build the file around the occupation.

Qualification names and job titles in Indonesian records are only one part of an occupation review. Duties, dates, employment evidence and the assessing authority may also matter.

Review assessment checkpoints
Skills assessment and English evidence

Keep the claims consistent from EOI to visa application.

Start with the relevant ANZSCO occupation where applicable. Then identify the assessing authority, qualification records, transcripts, employment references, English evidence and any professional registration or licensing requirement.

  • Nominated occupation and actual duties
  • Qualifications and academic transcripts
  • Employment history with dates and supporting records
  • Skills assessment outcome
  • English test result or applicable evidence category
  • Professional registration where required

What an assessment reviews

Concrete questions are more useful than broad promises.

Use this list to organise your documents before deciding whether the relevant information is about skilled migration, sponsorship or study.

Personal profile

  • Age and passport
  • Current country of residence
  • Family members included
  • Previous Australian visa history

Skilled profile

  • Occupation and ANZSCO match
  • Qualifications and employment
  • Skills assessment status
  • English test results and points score

Study profile

  • Proposed CRICOS course
  • CoE status and provider
  • Genuine Student evidence
  • Previous visa refusals where relevant
