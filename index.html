Points tested migration

How SkillSelect works

SkillSelect is used for subclasses 189, 190 and 491. The current published minimum points threshold for these points tested visas is 65 points. Reaching 65 points does not guarantee an invitation to apply.

  1. Identify the relevant skilled occupation

    Match your actual duties and qualifications to an occupation that is relevant to the visa pathway.

  2. Check occupation requirements

    Review the relevant skilled occupation list, assessing authority and any occupation specific criteria.

  3. Obtain a skills assessment

    Submit the documents requested by the assessing authority for your nominated occupation.

  4. Confirm English language evidence

    Check which English evidence is accepted for the visa and how your evidence must be provided.

  5. Calculate the points score

    Review age, qualifications, employment history, English and other points test factors.

  6. Submit an Expression of Interest

    Create and submit an EOI through SkillSelect with claims you can later support with evidence.

  7. Seek nomination where relevant

    Consider state or territory nomination for subclass 190 or a relevant subclass 491 pathway.

  8. Receive an invitation to apply

    An EOI is not a visa application. You must receive an invitation before applying through the invited pathway.

  9. Lodge through ImmiAccount

    Submit the visa application within the invitation period and follow the relevant application instructions.

  10. Provide supporting evidence

    Upload evidence for your identity, claims, health, character and any other requested criteria.

Invitation scores are not fixed by this page. State or territory nomination criteria and invitation settings can change, so a points score of 65 should not be treated as a promise of invitation.

Evidence before invitation

Skills assessment and English evidence

Indonesian qualifications and employment records should be reviewed against the nominated occupation, not only against a job title. The relevant assessing authority depends on the occupation and visa stream.

What the review covers

Build the occupation file around evidence.

Start with the ANZSCO occupation where applicable, then identify the assessing authority, qualification documents, transcripts, employment references and any registration or licensing requirement.

  • Nominated occupation and ANZSCO duties where applicable
  • Qualifications, transcripts and course records
  • Employment history, dates, duties and evidence of payment where requested
  • Skills assessment outcome and validity for the relevant visa stage
  • English language evidence and any applicable exemption category
  • Professional registration or licensing where the occupation requires it

Study in Australia from Indonesia

Student visa subclass 500

For Indonesian students and graduates comparing Australian university, VET or other registered courses, the application should connect the course choice with prior education, the provider, CoE, Genuine Student responses and supporting evidence.

CRICOSCoEGS requirementOSHC

  1. Select a CRICOS registered course and provider

    Use CRICOS to confirm that the intended course and education provider are registered for overseas students.

  2. Meet admission requirements

    Check the provider's academic, English and course entry requirements before accepting an offer.

  3. Receive an offer

    Read the course details, conditions and enrolment instructions in the provider's offer.

  4. Complete enrolment requirements

    Complete the provider steps required before a Confirmation of Enrolment can be issued.

  5. Obtain the Confirmation of Enrolment

    Provide a valid CoE for each intended course as required by the Student visa application.

  6. Arrange Overseas Student Health Cover

    Arrange OSHC or confirm the exemption category that applies to your circumstances.

  7. Prepare English and financial evidence

    Provide English language evidence and financial capacity evidence where required by the current instructions.

  8. Prepare Genuine Student responses

    Answer the GS questions in English using your real circumstances and supporting evidence.

  9. Lodge through ImmiAccount

    Submit the subclass 500 application online with the required identity and supporting documents.

  10. Complete additional requests

    Respond to any health, biometrics or document request that applies to your application.

A CoE supports evidence of enrolment. It does not guarantee that the Student visa will be granted.

Student visa evidence

Genuine Student requirement

The GS requirement asks whether you are a genuine applicant for entry and stay as a student and whether study in Australia is the primary reason for the Student visa application. Future skilled migration intentions do not automatically count against an applicant, but an Indonesian applicant's responses still need to be truthful, specific and evidence based.

Your current circumstances

Describe your present life in Indonesia, including employment, family and community context, living arrangements and economic circumstances.

Why this course and provider

Connect the selected Australian course and provider to your previous qualification, work history and stated academic or professional plan.

Understanding the proposed study

Show that you have checked the course structure, location, assessment style, study expectations and provider information.

Value of the course

Explain the knowledge or qualification sought and how it relates to future employment prospects in Indonesia or another country.

Previous education

Set out Indonesian and other prior qualifications, dates and institutions, and explain any change in academic direction with records.

Current employment

Describe your role, employer, duties and career position. Use employment evidence that matches the written response.

Economic circumstances

Explain who will fund tuition and living costs, the source of funds and which documents support the financial explanation.

Immigration history and supporting evidence

Disclose relevant travel, visa applications, refusals or cancellations and attach documents that support the facts provided.

Do not copy a model answer or create facts that are not part of your circumstances. GS responses should be written in your own words and supported by evidence.

Indonesian applicant questions

Country specific FAQ

Can I apply for subclass 189 from Indonesia?

Eligible applicants may submit a SkillSelect EOI from outside Australia. You still need the relevant occupation, skills assessment, English evidence, points score and invitation before lodging a visa application.

How should an Indonesian qualification be prepared for a skills assessment?

Check the assessing authority for the nominated occupation and prepare the qualification certificate, transcript, identity documents and employment evidence it requests. Do not assume one authority applies to every occupation.

What is the difference between subclass 189 and subclass 190 for Indonesian professionals?

Subclass 189 is a points tested independent visa. Subclass 190 is a permanent visa that requires state or territory nomination. Both use SkillSelect and an invitation process.

What should I check before seeking state nomination through subclass 190?

Check the current criteria published by the state or territory, then compare them with your occupation, skills assessment, English evidence, work history and EOI claims.

Can an Australian employer sponsor an Indonesian professional under subclass 482?

An Australian employer may be able to sponsor a skilled worker if the employer, nomination, occupation and applicant meet the Skills in Demand requirements. A job offer alone does not guarantee sponsorship or grant.

What is a CoE for a Student visa application from Indonesia?

A Confirmation of Enrolment is evidence of enrolment in a full time course registered on CRICOS. The CoE must be valid as required when the Student visa application is assessed.

What evidence can support Genuine Student responses from Indonesia?

Use real evidence about your current circumstances, previous education, employment, economic circumstances, course choice and immigration history. The response should explain your facts rather than repeat a model answer.

Does an Australian course guarantee a skilled visa later?

No. A Student visa, CoE or Australian qualification does not guarantee permanent residence. Any later subclass 485, skilled or employer sponsored application must meet the requirements in force at that time.
