Privacy.

How enquiries and personal information are handled on this ANZ Migrate website.

Website publisherANZANZ Migrate

Your information

The homepage has a contact form collecting your name, email address, phone number, country of residence and optional additional notes. No documents, photographs or file attachments are collected.

If secure SMTP email delivery is configured and you consent to submit, those details are emailed to the destination inbox configured for this website. Email providers and receiving mailboxes may store these messages. This website does not operate an applicant database or intentionally retain form submissions. A successful submission means the sending mail server accepted the message; it does not guarantee final inbox delivery.

Do not send passport numbers, account numbers or other highly sensitive information in the notes field.

Third-party services may load when the page is viewed, including Google Fonts and GSAP from public content delivery networks. When live chat is enabled, Chatwoot may process messages, browser identifiers and technical request data. These services may process information under their own terms.

Official sources

Use the Department of Home Affairs for visa requirements, SkillSelect and ImmiAccount instructions. Use CRICOS to check registered education providers and courses for overseas students.
